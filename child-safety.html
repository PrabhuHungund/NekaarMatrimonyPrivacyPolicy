Child Safety Standards

App Name: Nekaar Matrimony

Last Updated: May 11, 2026

Nekaar Matrimony is committed to providing a safe environment for our community. We have a zero-tolerance policy regarding the exploitation or abuse of children.

1. Prohibited Content and Conduct

We strictly prohibit the following on our platform:

  • The sharing, uploading, or distribution of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Content that depicts the sexual exploitation or abuse of minors.
  • Behavior intended to solicit, groom, or harm minors.

2. Reporting Mechanisms

Users are encouraged to report any content or behavior that violates these child safety standards. Reports can be made via:

  • The "Report User" button found on every profile within the app.
  • Direct email to our safety team at the address listed below.

3. Enforcement and Action

Upon receiving a valid report, we will:

  • Immediately remove the offending content.
  • Permanently ban the accounts involved in child safety violations.
  • Coordinate with law enforcement and report illegal material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or relevant local authorities.
